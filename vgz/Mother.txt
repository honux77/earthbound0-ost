Most of the tracks are unoptimized. I recommend

each VGM file (if you use -justtmr, the result
is minimal...).

Most of track names was taken from the a
NSF + playlist. Hopefully, the NSF order is
the correct order of gameplay.

MOTHER MUSIC REVISITED [DELUXE EDITION].

names in all-caps, while those that do not (or
have conflicting names) retain the NSF titles,
with the album's title in brackets where
